# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to providing a welcoming and professional environment. We expect all participants to:

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions
- Focus on technical merit and evidence-based arguments
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Prioritize the project's best interest

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and addressed appropriately.
