## Notes on completeness

This register enumerates 19 distinct claims. Claims C1–C7 and C18–C19 are grounded entirely in
the SPY-v1 primary artifacts. Claims C8–C14 and C16 are grounded in the robustness/characterization
artifacts and are explicitly scoped as such. C15 is a methodological/code-behavior claim
verifiable independent of which artifacts it happens to affect. C17 is the one claim in this
entire pack that is a pure external citation, not a claim this pack's authors verified themselves
— it is marked as such everywhere it appears (`SOURCE-GATE.md` §11, and nowhere else; it is
deliberately not repeated or leaned on elsewhere in `TECHNICAL-PAPER.md`'s own argument).

No claim in this register asserts forecasting power, trading edge, or statistical significance
beyond what its evidentiary basis directly supports; see `CLAIM-REDTEAM.md` for the adversarial
check of that assertion.
